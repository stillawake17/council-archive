Decision details

Supply of Topsoil for Bristol City Council Cemeteries

  • Find out more about this issue

Decision Maker: Director: Management of Place

Decision status: Recommendations Approved

Is Key decision?: No

Is subject to call in?: No

Purpose:

We are seeking to let a new Contract, under competition, for the provision of topsoil and mix soil for both backfilling and grounds maintenance for Bristol Cemeteries.

Decision:

Approval to award a contract to ETM Ltd following a Request to Quote process from 1 December 2026 to 30 November 2029 for the provision of topsoil and mix soil for both backfilling and grounds maintenance for Bristol Cemeteries.

Alternative options considered:

Routes to market were considered but it was felt due to there being a small number of suppliers capable of carrying out the service required, the decision was made to opt for a Request to Quote process via engagement with a range of local suppliers.
