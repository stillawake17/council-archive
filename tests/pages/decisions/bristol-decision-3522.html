Decision details

Public Participation and Conduct Procedure for Public Meetings

  • Find out more about this issue

Decision Maker: Director: Legal and Democratic Services (MO & SIRO)

Decision status: Recommendations Approved

Is Key decision?: No

Is subject to call in?: No

Purpose:

As part of the judicial review of the decision taken by the council to exclude members of the public from Council meetings and City Hall for six months the Council agreed to publish a Public Participation and Conduct Procedure for Public Meetings.

Decision:

To adopt the procedure annexed to this decision and publish it on the Council website.

Alternative options considered:

The Council must publish a procedure as part of the terms of settlement with the Claimants.
