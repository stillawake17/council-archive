Decision details

Hyper-V 3-tier Infrastructure Platform Provision

  • Find out more about this issue

Decision Maker: Director: Policy, Strategy and Digital

Decision status: Recommendations Approved

Is Key decision?: Yes

Is subject to call in?: No

Purpose:

The existing infrastructure (VxRail platform) is outdated and becoming increasingly expensive to license and support. It is therefore proposed to transition to a new platform Hyper-V.

Decision:

For the Director: Policy, Strategy

Alternative options considered:

1. Discontinue the service. This is not viable due to the business-critical nature of the infrastructure.
2. Continue with VxRail.
